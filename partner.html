How It Works

From First Message to Launch

Four steps, start to finish. Most partnerships are agreed within two to three weeks.

01

Reach Out

Tell us about your organisation and what you'd like to support — a fund, an event, or a track.

02

We Match a Track

Our team pairs you with the programme or sponsorship tier that fits your goals and budget.

03

Co-Create the Plan

We agree on deliverables, timelines, and how your organisation will be recognised.

04

Launch & Celebrate

Your partnership goes live — and we celebrate the impact together, publicly and proudly.
