Welcome

Kenya's Home for Twins & Multiples

Kenya Twins Association is the country's dedicated home for twins, triplets, and all multiple births. Since 2023, we've been building a community where twins connect, parents find support, and every multiple birth story is celebrated.

As an affiliate of Africa Twins Association and Global Twins & Multiple Birth Organizations, we're part of a worldwide movement doubling love and sharing care.

Our Mission

What We Do

01

Community Building

We bring twins and parents together through events, support groups, and the annual World Twins Day celebration — creating bonds that last a lifetime.

02

Empowerment

Through team-building, mentorship, and advocacy, we help twins and multiples thrive in school, work, and life — turning unique challenges into strengths.

03

Awareness

We educate the public on the needs of multiple births and fight stigma around twinning and poverty, ensuring every family feels seen and supported.

Our Story

About Kenya Twins Association

Founded 2023

Kenya Twins Association was founded in 2023 with a clear mission: to unite and empower twins and multiple births across Kenya.

We recognized that twins and multiples share a unique bond and unique challenges. KETA was created to provide a supportive community, advocate for their needs, and celebrate the joy of multiple births.

Our Global Affiliation

KETA is proud to be an affiliate of Africa Twins Association and Global Twins & Multiple Birth Organizations. This connection allows us to share best practices, access resources, and ensure Kenyan twins are represented on the global stage.

Through community building, empowerment, and advocacy, we help twins and multiples thrive in school, work, and life — fighting stigma and celebrating what makes multiple births extraordinary.
